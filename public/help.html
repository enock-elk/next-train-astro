Having trouble opening the app?

This rescue page works even when the main app is stuck, unstyled, or our servers are having a bad day.

Try this first

  1. Pull down to refresh, or close the tab and open Next Train again.
  2. If it still looks broken, tap Reset saved app data below - this clears an old cached copy (common after a big update).
  3. Still stuck? Send your contact details or WhatsApp / email us. We’ll reply when we can.
On iPhone: open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen again after reset. Or Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data (this clears other sites too).

More technical tips
  1. Android Chrome: menu → Delete browsing data → Cached images and files for this site, then reopen.
  2. If you installed the app icon, remove it and reopen from the browser once after reset.
  3. Check Cloudflare status if the whole internet feels broken.
