Having trouble opening the app?

This page still works when the main app is stuck or looking unfinished. Try the steps below.

Try this first

  1. Pull down to refresh, or close the app and open Next Train again.
  2. If it still looks broken, tap Reset saved app data below. That clears an old saved copy, which is common after a big update.
  3. Still stuck? Leave your contact details, or WhatsApp / email us. We’ll reply when we can.
On iPhone: open the site in Safari, then Share, then Add to Home Screen again after reset. Or Settings, Safari, Clear History and Website Data (this clears other sites too).
